The system

Lines, arcs & triangles

Every glyph is a skeleton of straight lines and true circular arcs. Bowls are full circles or stadiums; terminals are cut flat, square to the stroke.

One monoline

A single 60-unit stroke runs through capitals, lowercase, figures, and symbols. Joins are mitred up to a ratio of 4, so A, V, W, and M keep needle-sharp apexes.

Triangular dots

The period, colon, i and j dots, umlauts, and dot accents are solid triangles. Capitals stand 700 units tall over a 480-unit x-height with 720-unit ascenders.

Spacing & kerning

Every glyph gets 58-unit sidebearings. Class kerning (__KERN__ pairs) is measured from glyph profiles across all base and accented letters.
